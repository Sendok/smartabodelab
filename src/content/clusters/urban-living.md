Urban homes create a different design problem: less space, more shared infrastructure, and often less freedom to modify heating, wiring, doors, or exterior equipment.

Our urban-living coverage prioritizes reversible upgrades, compact setups, measurable comfort improvements, and building-aware energy decisions.
