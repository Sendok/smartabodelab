You do not need professional equipment to identify the most obvious energy waste in an apartment. Start with behavior, schedules, and the building envelope.

## Step 1: inspect always-on loads

List devices that remain powered around the clock: entertainment systems, desktop computers, routers, kitchen appliances, and chargers.

## Step 2: check windows and doors

Look for obvious drafts, damaged seals, and curtains blocking radiators or HVAC airflow.

## Step 3: review heating and cooling schedules

A thermostat schedule that matches occupancy often has more impact than adding another connected device. If your apartment uses a compatible central system, our [smart thermostat savings guide](/articles/smart-thermostat-savings-guide) explains where the efficiency opportunity actually comes from.

## Conclusion

Use the audit to create a short action list. Fix the highest-confidence sources of waste first, then measure whether your utility usage changes.
