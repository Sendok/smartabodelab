A good smart plug should be boringly reliable. Fast onboarding is useful, but long-term stability, local behavior, and meaningful energy data matter more.

## Reliability comes first

A plug that frequently disconnects creates more friction than value. Test recovery after power loss and router restarts.

## Energy monitoring

Look for current wattage, historical consumption, and exportable data if measurement is a primary reason for buying.

## Privacy and platform support

Prefer clear account requirements, predictable cloud dependence, and support for the ecosystems you already use. If protocol labels are confusing, the [Matter vs. Zigbee guide](/articles/matter-vs-zigbee) explains what each technology actually controls.

## Conclusion

Choose the smallest feature set that solves the actual automation or monitoring problem in your home.
