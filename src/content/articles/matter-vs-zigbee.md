Matter and Zigbee solve different parts of the smart-home interoperability problem. Understanding that distinction makes buying decisions much easier.

Matter is an application-layer standard designed to improve interoperability across major ecosystems. Zigbee is a mature low-power mesh networking technology used by many sensors, lights, and hubs.

## The practical difference

Matter is about a common language between devices and platforms. Zigbee is about how many low-power devices communicate within a local mesh network.

## When Zigbee still makes sense

Zigbee remains attractive for dense sensor networks, mature device ecosystems, and installations where a capable local hub already exists.

## What to prioritize when buying

Focus on the ecosystem you already use, local control, firmware support, bridge requirements, and whether a device still performs its core function if the internet is unavailable. Our [smart plug feature guide](/articles/smart-plugs-tested) shows how those protocol decisions translate into a common everyday device category.

## Conclusion

Do not treat protocol labels as a quality guarantee. Reliability, software support, and good automations matter more than the logo printed on the box.
