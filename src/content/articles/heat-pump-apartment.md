Heat pumps can work well in apartments, but the installation path depends heavily on building rules, exterior access, electrical capacity, and climate.

## Mini-split systems

Mini-splits can provide efficient heating and cooling, but they usually require landlord, condo board, or building approval because an outdoor unit and wall penetration may be involved.

## Portable heat pumps

Portable systems reduce installation complexity but can have lower seasonal performance and require careful venting.

## What to verify first

Check building permissions, electrical service, condensate routing, noise limits, and whether incentives apply to your exact equipment category. If thermostat control is part of the upgrade, review [how smart thermostat savings actually work](/articles/smart-thermostat-savings-guide) before assuming automation will produce a fixed percentage of savings.

## Conclusion

The best apartment heat-pump solution is the one that fits both the building constraints and the expected heating load.
